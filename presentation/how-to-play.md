Two squads of two heroes, Azure and Coral, meet on a 3D tabletop. You don't move the miniatures one by one: you give each hero orders, then watch the whole round play out by itself.

## Winning a match

- **Hold the relays.** At the end of each round, a squad that is alone on a relay or on a tile directly beside it (not diagonally) scores 1 point for it. A contested relay scores nothing.
- **Reach 8 points**, or **put both opposing heroes out of action**.
- After **12 rounds**, the higher score wins. Equal scores are a draw.

The included battlefield, The Terraces, has three relays down the middle, with obstacles and cover tiles on both sides.

## One round, step by step

1. **Equip your heroes (before round one only).** Balanced kit, Reinforced plates (+5 endurance, −1 movement), Amplifier (+2 power, −3 endurance) or Thruster boots (+1 movement, −1 power). Equipment is locked once the first round starts.
2. **Pick a tactical priority.** *Hold the relays* heads for a free relay and defends it. *Take the initiative* goes looking for a target. *Stay in cover* moves to the nearest free cover tile and fires from there. *Support the squad* puts healing first: a healer moves within reach of the most injured ally. A hero ordered to take the initiative never stops to heal.
3. **Pick a target priority.** *Nearest* or *Weakest* (the enemy with the least endurance left).
4. **Destination (optional).** A hero with a destination walks there instead of following their priority; it clears once they arrive. Movement is orthogonal, and obstacles and other miniatures cannot be crossed.
5. **Resolve the round.** Initiative order changes every round and favours faster heroes. Each hero moves, then automatically heals, attacks the first enemy in range and in line of sight, or guards if nobody is in reach.
6. **Read the log.** The battle log lists every move, roll, hit, miss, heal and point scored, with the numbers behind it.

## How a roll works

- Each hero rolls their proficiency die (d4 to d12; the four included heroes use a d10) together with a heroic d6, and keeps the higher result. A die that shows its maximum is rolled once more and added. A double 1 always fails.
- An attack needs **4**. Cover adds 2 against shots from more than one tile away, and super speed adds 1 against a hero who moved at least 2 tiles this round.
- Damage is fixed: the attacker's power, minus armor, minus 2 if the target is inside an allied kinetic field, minus 2 if the target is guarding. It never goes below zero.
- Beating the number needed by 4 or more is a **great success**: +2 damage, and a target that takes damage and stays up is **shaken**. A shaken hero drops their guard, and a shaken field bearer stops protecting anyone. They recover at their next action, with −2 to that roll.
- Each hero has endurance points. A hero at zero is out of action for the rest of the match.
