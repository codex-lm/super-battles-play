Two squads of two heroes, Azure and Coral, meet on a 3D tabletop. You don't move the miniatures one by one: you give each hero orders, then watch the whole round play out by itself.

## Winning a match

- **Hold the relays.** At the end of each round, a squad that is alone on a relay or on a tile directly beside it (not diagonally) scores 1 point for it. A contested relay scores nothing.
- **Reach 8 points**, or **put both opposing heroes out of action**.
- After **12 rounds**, the higher score wins. Equal scores are a draw.

The included battlefield, The Terraces, has three relays down the middle, with obstacles and cover tiles on both sides.

## One round, step by step

1. **Equip your heroes (before round one only).** Balanced kit, Reinforced plates (+5 endurance, −1 movement), Amplifier (+2 power, −3 endurance) or Thruster boots (+1 movement, −1 power). Equipment is locked once the first round starts.
2. **Choose each hero's action.** _Attack_, _Guard_ (give up the attack to reduce damage by 2) or, for a healer, _Heal_. _Automatic_ lets the hero follow their tactical priority instead.
3. **Name a target (optional).** Pick the exact enemy to attack or ally to heal. A named target is never silently swapped: if it falls, gets out of range or out of sight, the hero guards and the log says why. With no named target, the hero picks a valid one.
4. **Set the movement.** Click a tile for a destination, or choose _Hold position_. With no destination, an Attack or Heal order moves just far enough to reach range and line of sight. Movement is orthogonal, and obstacles and other miniatures cannot be crossed.
5. **Check the plan.** Blue markers show each hero's route and endpoint, a dashed line shows the target, and the panel spells it out. A crimson warning means the route ends next to an enemy Killcam, who may intercept. The plan uses current positions, so heroes acting earlier can change it.
6. **Resolve the round.** Initiative order changes every round and favours faster heroes. Each hero moves, may be intercepted, then attacks, heals or guards.
7. **Read the log, then replan.** The battle log lists every move, roll, hit, miss, heal and point scored, with the numbers behind it. Orders stay in place until you change them; a destination clears on arrival.

Under _Autopilot & equipment_, each hero also keeps a tactical priority (hold the relays, take the initiative, stay in cover, support the squad) and a target priority (nearest or weakest), used by the Automatic action and when no target is named.

## How a roll works

- Each hero rolls their proficiency die (d4 to d12; Killcam uses a d12, the four other included heroes a d10) together with a heroic d6, and keeps the higher result. A die that shows its maximum is rolled once more and added. A double 1 always fails.
- An attack needs **4**. Cover adds 2 against shots from more than one tile away, and super speed adds 1 against a hero who moved at least 2 tiles this round.
- Damage is fixed: the attacker's power, minus armor, minus 2 if the target is inside an allied kinetic field, minus 2 if the target is guarding. It never goes below zero. Killcam's interception is an ordinary attack, and the hero she strikes has not acted yet, so it has no guard.
- Beating the number needed by 4 or more is a **great success**: +2 damage, and a target that takes damage and stays up is **shaken**. A shaken hero drops their guard, and a shaken field bearer stops protecting anyone. They recover at their next action, with −2 to that roll.
- Each hero has endurance points. A hero at zero is out of action for the rest of the match.
