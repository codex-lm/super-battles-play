Pick the match type at the top of the battlefield. Solo and shared-screen play need no account.

## Solo against the automaton

You command Azure; the automaton gives Coral's orders each round. It heals when it can, goes for the relays during the first four rounds, then switches to attacking. It always targets the weakest of your heroes it can reach.

## Two players, one screen

Plan Azure and Coral in turn with the two squad tabs, then press Resolve both squads. Best with a friend next to you and one honest promise not to peek.

## Two players in a LOB7 room

Choose a room in the LOB7 player, then select the LOB7 room mode. The host commands Azure and their partner Coral. The host sets up squads, equipment and battlefield before connecting; the partner plays Coral as the host set it up. After that, each player gives their own heroes' orders, and the round resolves once both have locked them in. Replay and New match are not available in a room. This mode is still being tested with two real accounts.

## Language, sound and saves

- Your language choice is remembered.
- Power sound effects start with your first action. Music is off until you turn it on, and there is a volume slider.
- The current match is saved in your browser. Export match and Restore match let you keep it as a file or pick it up elsewhere.
