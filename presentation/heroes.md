Five original heroes come with the game. By default, **Bastion and Volta** form the Azure squad and **Sillage and Suture** the Coral squad. Before round one, the workshop lets you swap any of them for another included hero or for your own character. The first four roll a d10 proficiency die, Killcam a d12.

## Bastion, Guardian

_The last line of defense is always one step ahead._

**Kinetic field.** Passive: damage to Bastion and to allies directly beside him (not diagonally) is reduced by 2. Two kinetic fields on the same hero do not stack (custom heroes can carry one too), and the field is interrupted while Bastion is shaken.

- Endurance 26
- Power 6, range 1, movement 3, armor 2

## Volta, Striker

_One spark can change everything._

**Piercing ray.** Her shots ignore 1 point of armor. Cover still makes her miss more often, and force fields and guard still protect the target.

- Endurance 18
- Power 7, range 5, movement 3, armor 0

## Suture, Support

_No one gets left behind._

**Repair nanites.** Uses the hero's action, at range 4 with line of sight: restores 4 endurance, or 6 on a great success. One attempt every two rounds. Heroes who are out of action cannot be brought back.

- Endurance 20
- Power 4, range 4, movement 3, armor 1

## Sillage, Scout

_Already elsewhere. Already ahead._

**Super speed.** After moving at least 2 tiles, attacks against Sillage need 1 more to hit until the end of the round. Obstacles still block the way.

- Endurance 18
- Power 6, range 2, movement 5, armor 0

## Killcam, Interceptor

_Threat assessed. Civilians protected._

**Intercept.** Once per round, when an enemy ends its move right beside Killcam (not diagonally) after starting farther away, she strikes it with her blades before it can act. The strike is a normal attack that uses neither her move nor her action. A hero she puts out of action never acts; a hero she shakes still acts, with −2 on that roll. She can't intercept while shaken, and the orders panel warns you when a route ends next to her.

- Endurance 18
- Power 6, range 1, movement 4, armor 2
